# Readable source names

Runtime functions, arguments, variables and members have descriptive names
without native-address suffixes. For example, `MwApplyCurrentPlanetState()`
applies a world's local conditions to the campaign.

Use established BattleTech terms, but do not assume tabletop rules or invent a
meaning for an unknown field. Distinguish pilot identity, crew-roster position,
owned Mech, chassis specification, weapon kind and weapon mount.

Method descriptions should explain what happens to the company, Mech, weapon
or mission. Graphics and audio methods describe their drawing or sound purpose.
Detailed historical symbol mappings and native annotations are maintained only
in the reverse-engineering repository.
