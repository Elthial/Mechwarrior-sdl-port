# Unused geometry: what has actually been established

The model-bank survey identifies resources and their known animation or scene
references. All 780 shipped Mech shapes have recovered body-part and animation
membership. The tools expose that membership rather than treating each shape
as an independent whole BattleMech.

No Wasp or Wolverine has been positively identified as a surviving playable
model. Retained chassis names in campaign code are not enough to identify
otherwise unexplained geometry as one of those designs.

The survey is not proof that every miscellaneous resource is used in every
mission, or that every remaining unexplained resource was cut content. Padding,
alternate detail, shared shapes and unidentified metadata need to be distinguished
before assigning a development-history explanation.

The machine-readable survey remains research evidence, not an asset export.
Use the Models page to inspect geometry and the recovered catalogue labels;
keep unidentified shapes neutral until their purpose is established.
